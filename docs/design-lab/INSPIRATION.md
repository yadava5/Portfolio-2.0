# Design-lab inspiration inventory (Chrome tour, July 2026)

## Observed live (screenshots taken, real Chrome)

### daylightcomputer.com — the warmth benchmark
- Warmth comes from LIGHT IN IMAGERY, not flat cream: hero is golden-hour
  photography; the paper sections carry soft diagonal LIGHT RAYS raking across
  the background. A flat `#FAF6EF` div is NOT the move — light must feel real.
- Scroll-scrubbed word-by-word ink reveal ("Introducing *Daylight* — …") with
  ONE italic serif accent word — exactly our Ch-02 manifesto spec, proven live.
- Editorial serif headline over photography; tiny tracked-caps labels.
- **Steal:** light-ray treatment for our Layer 0 (sun as raking light, not
  radial gradient); the word-scrub cadence.

### robin-noguier.com
- Per-project full-viewport COLOR-WORLD takeover (slate panel slides over
  near-black carrying the case study). Discrete slides; ours (continuous
  day-arc) is the more original evolution of the same instinct.
- Giant display-serif name in muted slate blue on near-black; personality
  micro-copy (fake "dark patterns" toast) — wit humanizes.
- Velocity-reactive image warp/skew on scroll — memorable but floaty; SKIP
  (banned-adjacent).
- Right-edge dot rail for wayfinding.
- **Steal:** color-world-per-chapter conviction; micro-copy wit.

### press.stripe.com
- ONE physical metaphor executed completely: books as literal 3D spines in a
  stack; scroll separates the stack; each spine textured, colored, dimensional.
  Warm near-black canvas + rich spine palette.
- Left-edge minimal tick rail appears on scroll (quiet wayfinding).
- Nothing on the page is generic: no cards, no grids — the METAPHOR is the UI.
- **Steal:** commit to our pipeline/approval-gate metaphor as literally as
  Stripe commits to books. The red thread must BE the interface, not decor.

### dennissnellenberg.com
- Multilingual greeting preloader (Hello → स्वागत हे → Ciao). Famous, now
  widely copied — treat as cliché, do NOT copy. (Also violates our "no boot
  screens" spirit.) Site works fine in Chrome; didn't tour past preloader.

## Principles extracted (for the candidate mockups)
1. Warmth = light, not hex. Simulated raking light/grain beats flat cream.
2. One metaphor, total commitment (Stripe books ⇒ our pipeline-run + gate).
3. Color-world conviction per chapter — but continuous (day-arc), not slides.
4. Personality through micro-copy wit, not effects.
5. Editorial serif + tiny tracked mono labels is the confirmed voice.
6. Quiet edge rail wayfinding (ticks/numbers), appearing after the hero.

### niccolomiranda.com — "The Paper Portfolio" (toured live)
- Full newspaper metaphor: masthead, dateline, column rules, drop caps, boxed
  dashed-border testimonial "clippings", giant condensed display type.
- Mid-scroll the entire sheet ROTATES like paper turned on a desk.
- A literal POSTAGE STAMP graphic (orange sunburst rays + signature +
  name/date fields) — validates our approval-stamp motif.
- Transpose (don't copy): newspaper→ ML run-log/research dossier; stamps =
  approval gates; clay sunburst = our day-arc sun.

### Research digest (agent, July 2026)
- 2025–26 winner formula: TWO colors, ONE big idea, motion as the brand
  (Pacôme #0a0a0a/#fafafa, Elliott, Olha). Maximalist WebGL is now the outlier.
- MindMarket (SOTM Dec 2025): drawn line leads the narrative = our red thread,
  award-validated (Rive + GSAP).
- Toolset verdicts: SplitText 2025 rewrite (a11y-safe masked line reveals),
  DrawSVG for the thread/diagrams, Flip for list↔grid morph (Pacôme move),
  CSS cross-document View Transitions for index→case-study continuity
  (Chrome/Safari, degrades in Firefox), ONE variable-wght kinetic headline
  axis. Motion/anime.js skipped (second engine). Rive = consider later.
- Ten stealable moves logged; watch-out: keep any high-chroma accent on warm
  paper, never near-black (Lando lime = one hex from banned console look).
