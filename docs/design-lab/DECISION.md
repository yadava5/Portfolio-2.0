## CHOSEN: "The Working Paper" — C's skeleton in A's living world, B's dusk

C wins the comparison but forfeits our biggest atmospheric weapon (the day-arc).
The unified direction:

1. **Layout DNA from C**: masthead-scale hero (~10rem Fraunces, 0.95 leading),
   working-paper frame ("a working paper on trust & machinery"), print apparatus
   (¶ kickers, folios NN/07, datelines, fig. captions, footnote that resolves the
   hero's superscript, colophon), vast whitespace, no cards/surfaces on day pages.
2. **The line from C** (= the plan's Red Thread, grammar proven): ONE continuous
   hand-wobbled INK line travels the whole page — underlines the hero accent,
   becomes each chapter's hand-drawn diagram, crosses every folio rule.
   Ink during day; passed nodes tick PINE; **clay is reserved for decisions** —
   gate moments and the final distressed APPROVED stamp (C's discipline).
3. **The world from A/plan**: day-arc background travel dawn→dusk (oklch,
   scroll-scrubbed), ≤5% grain, raking light made PERCEPTIBLE (Daylight-style),
   full type system (Fraunces opsz/SOFT + Newsreader + Fragment Mono lowercase).
4. **Dusk from B**: after the Ch-06 flip the paper becomes lamplight ledger
   (waypoint-06/07 tokens already encode B's browns) — B's surface language
   makes the flip MEANINGFUL: the working paper's day ends, the ledger closes.
5. **Wit in the apparatus**, never effects: footnotes, "still in review" honesty,
   colophon ("set by hand · two inks. one line. seven chapters.").

NO-LIST status: all three candidates audited clean by their builders (trailing
HTML comments); the synthesis inherits only audited elements.

Master-plan status: concept VALIDATED by competition — thread/day-arc/gate all
survive; what changes is execution grammar: bigger type, print apparatus,
hand-drawn line, one-clay discipline, working-paper voice.
